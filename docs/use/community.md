# Bookings, visitors, parcels

## Facility bookings

Each bookable facility (hall, barbecue area, court, guest flat) has its own rules: opening hours, maximum duration, how far ahead one may book, bookings per unit per month, and an optional fee. A booking inside the rules **confirms instantly**. A fee becomes a charge on the account of whoever booked. The manager can cancel bookings and block dates.

## Visitors

1. **Pre-registration.** A resident enters a guest's name, plate and time window and gets a **QR code** to send to the guest.
2. **QR admission.** The guard scans the code; if it is valid now and for this gate, the guard admits directly and the visit is logged against the unit. Where a gate is automated through the [on-site agent](../operate/agent.md), the gate can act on it.
3. **No QR.** For an unexpected visitor the guard enters the name (and plate) and the unit; the resident gets an approve-or-deny request and answers from the web or the app.
4. **Guard console.** A booth page for a tablet or PC: today's expected visitors, search by name or plate, arrived and left, walk-ins, the approval request button, and parcels.
5. **Recurring visitors.** A standing permission for a named person (cleaner, carer) on chosen days and hours, with a long-lived QR the resident can revoke.

## Parcels

The guard logs a delivery against a unit, the resident is notified, and the guard marks it collected and by whom. Photos and signatures are not required.
